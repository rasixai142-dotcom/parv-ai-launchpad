# AI Mastery Hub

Build a high-converting live webinar registration landing page for the AI 







Mastery Program by Parv Infosoft, founded by Kaushal Tiwari (Surat, Gujarat). 







This landing page sells registration to a FREE/low-fee live webinar (the 







top-of-funnel), not the paid course tiers directly — the paid tiers (Rs. 4,500 







/ Rs. 8,500 / Rs. 20,000) are sold during the webinar itself.







This should be modeled after the proven structure and psychological patterns 







used by 13 currently-running, high-traffic competitor pages in the exact same 







category (AI education/workshop funnels selling into a backend paid program), 







including Be10x, Outskill, Priyank Singh's AI Agents workshop, MAD ADS AI UGC 







workshop, House of Virality, FutureProofIndia, and others.







PAGE STRUCTURE (in this order):







1. HERO SECTION







   - Headline: outcome-focused, specific, curiosity-driven (not generic "learn 







     AI" — reference exact tasks/results, e.g. "Build a working AI automation 







     live, in this session")







   - Subheadline with 2-3 concrete outcomes







   - Original price struck through, discounted price shown 







     (e.g., "~~Rs. 999~~ Rs. 99")







   - Date, time, format (Live, on Zoom, duration)







   - Primary CTA button







   - Countdown timer to registration deadline







2. "IS THIS YOU?" / PROBLEM AGITATION SECTION







   - List specific, relatable frustrations the target audience currently faces 







     (not generic pain points — specific scenarios they'd recognize instantly)







3. COST/VALUE COMPARISON (if applicable to positioning)







   - A simple two-column comparison: "the old expensive/slow way" vs. 







     "the new way this teaches" — with real numbers if possible







4. WHAT YOU'LL LEARN / SNEAK PEEK SECTION







   - Specific, numbered list of exactly what will be covered







   - Explicitly state that something will be BUILT LIVE during the session 







     (a real AI workflow/automation/output — not just explained)







5. WHO THIS IS FOR / WHO THIS IS NOT FOR







   - Positive qualifier list: types of people this benefits







   - Negative qualifier list: e.g., "not for those looking for passive 







     watching or shortcuts without doing the work" — this filters and also 







     builds credibility







6. BEFORE / AFTER TRANSFORMATION TABLE







   - Row-by-row contrast: "Before: [current state]" vs "After: [outcome state]"







   - 4-6 rows, specific and visual







7. MENTOR/FOUNDER SECTION







   - Photo + first-person bio for Kaushal Tiwari







   - Quantified credibility markers (specific numbers — avoid vague claims)







   - Personal, direct tone: "Hi, I'm Kaushal, and I'll be leading this session..."







8. BONUSES SECTION







   - Numbered list (e.g., "5+ Free Bonuses") each with an explicit rupee value







   - State total combined bonus value







   - Frame as "for live attendees only" to reinforce attendance







9. SOCIAL PROOF SECTION







   - If testimonials/ratings exist, include them with specific, outcome-focused 







     quotes (not generic praise)







   - If this is a new brand with no testimonials yet, replace with additional 







     founder transparency/credibility content instead of fabricating proof







10. URGENCY/SCARCITY SECTION







    - Limited seats messaging







    - Countdown timer repeated







    - "Register before [deadline] to unlock bonuses" framing







11. FAQ SECTION







    - Must address: exact timing, whether it's live, whether there's a 







      recording (decide and state clearly — either "no recording, live only" 







      to protect attendance, or "recording provided" to reduce registration 







      friction — pick one strategy and be explicit, don't leave ambiguous), 







      what to prepare, refund/support contact, "do I need coding/technical 







      experience" (explicitly say no)







12. FINAL CLOSING SECTION







    - Direct-response style closing (restate offer, price, urgency one final time)







    - Final CTA







STICKY ELEMENTS:







- Persistent CTA bar/button visible while scrolling







PRICING GUIDANCE:







- Test entry price in the Rs. 9-199 range rather than assuming one fixed number; 







  competitors in this exact category successfully use prices across this entire 







  range depending on positioning and backend offer size







DESIGN REQUIREMENTS:







- Mobile-first (primary traffic will be Instagram/Meta ads on mobile)







- Fast-loading, minimal heavy assets







- Single conversion goal — no navigation menu or distracting external links







- Visible headline + CTA without scrolling on mobile







TONE OF COPY:







- Direct-response marketing style: confident, specific, benefit-led







- Every claim should be as specific/quantified as possible (numbers, timeframes, 







  concrete outcomes) rather than vague/generic language







- Written for an Indian audience; can mix Hindi/Hinglish in testimonials or 







  informal sections, but keep headline/CTA copy in clear English for broad reach
